# Returns and refunds

Most items on mark8ly can be returned within 14 days of delivery. The exact window and condition rules depend on the seller and the category — check the order detail page for the policy that applies to your purchase.

## Eligibility

An item is returnable when **all** of these are true:

- It's within the return window shown on the order page.
- It's in its original condition with original packaging.
- It isn't one of the excluded categories (perishables, intimate apparel, customised goods, downloadable items).

If your order is mixed (e.g. one returnable item, one non-returnable), you can still return the eligible items independently — the system splits the refund.

## How to start a return

1. Open the order on your account page.
2. Tap **"Return or exchange"** next to the item.
3. Pick a reason. Some reasons (damaged, wrong item) qualify for free return shipping; others (changed mind) deduct return shipping from the refund.
4. Print the prepaid label and drop the parcel at any of the supported couriers.

## When does the refund land?

Once the seller receives and inspects the return:

- **UPI / wallet** — within 24 hours.
- **Credit / debit card** — within 5-7 business days, depending on your bank.
- **Cash on delivery** — credited to mark8ly store credit by default; you can switch to a bank transfer in account settings.

If the refund hasn't landed after the window above, contact support with your **order_id** and the **return ID** (visible on the return tracking page).

## What if the seller rejects my return?

Sellers can reject a return only if the item is outside the eligible condition (damaged in a way the original delivery wasn't, parts missing, evidence of use). If you disagree, raise a dispute from the return page within 48 hours — a mark8ly mediator reviews the evidence on both sides and makes the final call.
