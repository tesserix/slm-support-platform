# Tracking your order

Every order moves through five stages, and you can see the current one on the order detail page:

1. **Placed** — payment is captured, the seller has been notified.
2. **Confirmed** — the seller has accepted the order. Most sellers confirm within 2 hours.
3. **Packed** — the item is boxed and waiting for the courier pickup window.
4. **Shipped** — the courier has scanned the parcel. A tracking link is added to the page when this happens.
5. **Delivered** — the courier has confirmed delivery.

## Where's my tracking link?

Tracking links appear once the courier scans the parcel into their network — not when the seller marks "shipped" in their dashboard. There can be a 12-24 hour gap between those two events, especially over weekends.

If your order has been in **"Shipped"** for more than 48 hours without a tracking link, contact support with your **order_id** and we'll chase the seller.

## My tracking link isn't updating

Tracking events come from the courier's system, not from mark8ly. Some couriers update in real time, others batch their scans every 4-6 hours. If the link is showing "Out for delivery" but nothing changed after a day, the parcel is most likely with the local courier hub awaiting a redelivery attempt — file a ticket and we'll request an update.

## I never got my order

If the tracking says delivered but you don't have it:

1. Check with neighbours and your building's reception or mailroom.
2. Look for a "safe place" or "left with neighbour" note on the tracking page.
3. If both come up empty within 48 hours, raise a missing-delivery ticket. We dispute it with the courier and refund proactively if the dispute drags on.
