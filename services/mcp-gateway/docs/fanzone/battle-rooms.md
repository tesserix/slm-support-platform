# How live battle rooms work

A **battle room** is a private prediction lobby tied to a specific match. Everyone in the room locks in their predictions before the toss; once the match starts the room becomes a leaderboard you can watch in real time.

## Joining a room

You can join a battle room three ways:

1. **From the match card** — tap a live match, then "Join battle". You're slotted into a public room with strangers at your skill tier.
2. **From an invite link** — paste it into the FanZone web app and you skip the matchmaking step.
3. **From the IPL 2026 hub** — featured rooms for top matches show up with a join button.

## Room sizes and scoring caps

- Rooms hold **20-100 players**.
- The top 10% of the room split a bonus pot of 200 points (or 500 in featured rooms).
- Anyone in the room earns the 1.5× live-battle multiplier on predictions made before the toss.

## Why didn't I get into the room I clicked?

Two common reasons:

- **Toss already happened** — pre-toss predictions are locked from the moment the toss result is reported, so rooms close to new joiners at that point. You can still chat and watch but your predictions won't carry the multiplier.
- **Room filled up** — public rooms cap at 100. The app automatically routes you to the next-available room with similar players.

If you joined a room but it "kicks" you back to the match page, that's almost always a stale link. Refresh and try again from the live match card.
