# How points and scoring work in FanZone

Every prediction you make in FanZone is worth a base score that depends on what you predicted and how close you came to the real outcome.

## Base points per prediction type

- **Match winner** — 25 points for picking the winning team. 0 points otherwise.
- **Top run-scorer** — 40 points for picking the correct player. 10 points for picking any player who finished in the top three.
- **Total runs** — 30 points if you're within 10 runs of the actual total, scaled down to 0 at a 50-run gap.
- **Wicket count** — 20 points for guessing the right wicket count, 10 points for being within 1.

## Bonus multipliers

If you join a **live battle room** you earn 1.5× points on any prediction locked in before the toss. Predictions placed mid-innings don't get the multiplier — that's a deliberate trade-off between flexibility (you can change your mind) and reward (you had less risk).

Streak bonuses kick in after **three correct match-winner picks in a row**. Each subsequent correct pick is worth +10 points on top of the base, capped at +40. A wrong pick resets the streak.

## When points appear in your wallet

Points settle within **5 minutes of the match ending**. If you don't see your points after 30 minutes, the match scoring job may be retrying — check the status banner on the IPL 2026 page, then contact support.

The amounts you earn during a season also feed into the seasonal leaderboard (separate from your all-time total).
