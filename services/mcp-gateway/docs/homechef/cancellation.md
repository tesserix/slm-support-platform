# Cancelling a HomeChef order

You can cancel from the order page up until the chef starts cooking. After that the cancellation has consequences for both you and the chef, which is why the rules tighten with time.

## Cancellation windows

- **Before the chef accepts** — full refund, no questions.
- **Chef accepted, not yet cooking** — full refund. Cancel button still available on the order page.
- **Chef started cooking** — the cancel button disappears from the page. You can still cancel by raising a ticket, but a partial refund applies (typically 60-80%) to compensate the chef for ingredients already used.
- **Driver assigned and en route** — no cancellation. The order will be delivered. If you genuinely can't accept it, contact support and we'll work out a reasonable refund.

## I cancelled but I'm still being charged

Cancellations refund through the same channel the original payment used:

- UPI / wallet — within 24 hours
- Cards — 5-7 business days
- HomeChef store credit — within 1 hour (the fastest path; switchable to bank transfer in account settings)

If a refund hasn't landed past those windows, contact support with the **order_id** and we'll pull the refund log.

## The order disappeared from my list

We auto-hide cancelled orders from the active list after they fully refund, so a cancellation that processed cleanly will move to the "Past orders" tab within an hour. If you can't find it in either list within a day, the order_id is still searchable from the help screen — share it in a ticket and we'll surface the record.
