# Tracking your HomeChef order

HomeChef shows live ETAs in three stages once you place an order:

1. **Chef preparing** — the chef has accepted and the food is being made. Typical window is 20-35 minutes depending on the dish.
2. **Driver assigned** — a delivery partner has been matched. The map shows their live location from this point.
3. **Out for delivery** — the driver has picked the order up. The ETA tightens to a 5-minute window.

## My ETA keeps changing

HomeChef recalculates the ETA every few minutes based on the driver's current location, traffic conditions, and the chef's prep time. Small swings (±5 minutes) are normal, especially during peak dinner hours.

A **larger jump** (>15 minutes later) typically means one of:

- The chef accepted late or the kitchen got slammed with simultaneous orders.
- The original driver was re-routed and a new one was assigned.
- The order was batched with a nearby second order to optimise delivery.

If your order is more than 30 minutes past the original ETA, raise a ticket with the **order_id** and we'll either chase the driver or process a partial refund.

## The driver looks lost / stationary

The map updates every 30 seconds. If the driver looks frozen for longer than 2-3 minutes:

- Check the address on the order page — if it's wrong, tap **"Edit address"** and add a note. The driver's app pulls live updates.
- Call the driver via the in-app button (your number is masked).
- If you can't reach the driver and the dot hasn't moved for 10+ minutes, raise an "Order stuck" ticket. We can reassign to another driver.

## I never got my food

If delivery is marked complete but you didn't receive the order:

1. Check the order page for a "left at door" or "left with reception" note.
2. Confirm the address shown on the order matches yours.
3. If the order is genuinely missing, raise a **missing delivery ticket within 2 hours** of the delivery timestamp. We refund and dispute with the driver in parallel.
