# Food quality and refunds

If something went wrong with your order — cold food, wrong dish, missing item, damaged packaging — HomeChef refunds quickly. We don't ask you to send the food back.

## When to raise it

Quality complaints carry weight when they're raised **within 2 hours** of delivery. The chef and driver are still on shift, photos still show the food in its delivered state, and the chef's compliance score can be adjusted in real time.

Older complaints are still reviewable, but they go through a slower manual queue.

## What to include

The faster path through the queue:

- **A photo** of the issue (cold food in original packaging, wrong dish, etc.). The system OCR's the dish and matches against your order — automatic refunds for clear matches.
- **The order_id** (from the URL of the order page).
- A one-line description of the issue ("rice was completely cold" / "got chicken biryani instead of veg").

## What's refundable

- **Wrong / missing item** — full refund on the affected items, no questions asked.
- **Quality issue (cold, raw, etc.)** — refund of the affected dish; if more than half the order is affected, full order refund.
- **Damaged packaging** — refund on the affected dish.
- **Late delivery (>30 min over ETA)** — partial refund (typically 15-25%) on the delivery fee plus a credit for next order.

## What's not refundable

- Personal taste ("didn't like the flavour"). We surface this as feedback to the chef but don't refund.
- "Felt cold by the time I ate it" if the delivery itself was on time and you opened the order >20 minutes after arrival.

If you disagree with the decision the automated system makes, raise a follow-up ticket — a human reviews it within 4 hours during operating hours.
