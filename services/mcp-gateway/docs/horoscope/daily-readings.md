# How daily and transit readings are generated

Two kinds of readings appear in your feed each day:

- **Sun-sign daily** — a short general reading keyed to your sun sign. Same for every user with that sun sign.
- **Personal transit reading** — based on the planets' positions today relative to your birth chart. Different for every user.

Personal transit readings are the substantial ones — they consider current transits, your specific ascendant, and the active dasha period.

## Why are my readings different from my friend with the same sun sign?

Sun sign is one of about 12 factors in the personal reading. Two people sharing a sun sign but born on different days, times, or places will have completely different ascendants, moon signs, and dasha periods — which all weigh into the personal reading.

If you and a friend are getting identical readings every day, you're both seeing the *sun-sign daily* (free tier) and not the *personal transit* (premium tier).

## Today's reading mentions a transit I don't see in my chart

Transits are planet positions **today**, not in your birth chart. The reading flags when a current transit makes an aspect to a birth-chart planet — that's the moment the prediction is grounded in. If you're not seeing the transit, scroll down past your natal chart to the **Today's Transits** section.

## How accurate is "Today's lucky time"?

Lucky times are computed from the day's planetary hours (horas) modulated by your ascendant. They're indicative, not deterministic — treat them as a tie-breaker between two activities, not a hard schedule.

If you want stricter timing (a muhurta for a specific event), the **Personalised muhurta** feature under Premium consults your full chart and the event's nature; it's much more rigorous than the daily lucky-time hint.

## My reading mentions a dasha period I haven't entered

We compute your active dashas (planetary periods) from your birth time. If you've recently corrected your birth time, the dasha can shift by years; readings will reflect that immediately on the next refresh. If you think the dasha is wrong despite a correct birth time, double-check the Settings → Birth details page and contact support if the dates still look off.
